# POL-05 Escalation Rules for Delivery Performance
Owner: Head of Operations | Effective: 2018-01-01 | Status: fictional policy written for this project

## Purpose
Make sure delivery problems reach someone who can fix them quickly, with a
clear owner and deadline at each level.

## Escalation levels
- Level 1 - Regional Operations Lead: any SLA breach under POL-01. Must
  acknowledge within 1 working day and send a root-cause note within 5
  working days.
- Level 2 - Head of Logistics: a state on the watch list for a second month,
  or a quarter-over-quarter drop of more than 5 percentage points. Requires a
  recovery plan with weekly progress updates.
- Level 3 - VP Operations: the national on-time delivery rate is below target
  for two consecutive months. Weekly review meeting until recovered.

## Root-cause note
The root-cause note must show which sellers, product categories and states
contributed most to the drop, and whether actual delivery times got longer or
the estimated delivery dates got shorter. A note that only restates the drop is
sent back.

## De-escalation
A case is closed when the state or national rate is back at or above target
for two consecutive months. The owner at the current level confirms closure in
the monthly report.

## Exceptions
During peak season (see POL-08) Level 1 breaches are logged but the root-cause
note deadline is extended to 10 working days.
