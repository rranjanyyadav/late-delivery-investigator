# POL-01 Delivery SLA and On-Time Targets
Owner: Logistics Operations | Effective: 2018-01-01 | Status: fictional policy written for this project

## Purpose
Customers judge the marketplace by whether parcels arrive by the estimated
delivery date shown at checkout. This policy sets the on-time targets that
Logistics Operations is measured against and defines when a miss becomes an
SLA breach.

## Definitions
An order is on time when it is delivered to the customer on or before the
estimated delivery date (compared by calendar day). The on-time delivery rate
is the share of delivered orders that arrived on time. Canceled and
undelivered orders are excluded from the rate.

## Targets
- National target: on-time delivery rate of at least 92% per calendar month.
- State target: on-time delivery rate of at least 90% per state per calendar
  month, for every state with 300 or more delivered orders in that month.
- Quarterly target: a state's on-time delivery rate must not fall by more than
  3 percentage points compared with the previous quarter.

## SLA breach
A breach is recorded when a target is missed. A state that misses the state
target for two consecutive months, or that falls by more than 3 percentage
points quarter over quarter, is placed on the watch list and handled under
POL-05 Escalation Rules.

## Reporting
The Reporting & Analytics team publishes the on-time delivery rate by state
and month in the first three working days of each month. Breaches are flagged
in the report with the size of the miss in percentage points.
